# world-terrain (map generation, tile storage, pathfinding, terrain mesh, water, features, territory visuals, camera/touch)

## Summary
The map is random per player. Game.create picks a random seed (game.ts:147-152, `Math.floor(Math.random()*2**31)`; world.json map.seed is declared but never read) and calls generateMap(content, seed) (generator.ts:25-175). The generator builds three value-noise fields from core/noise.ts:28-42 (elevation, moisture, temperature). Each noise grid is sized by `size = max(width,height)` (generator.ts:29). Elevation is pulled down towards the map edges (`edgeFalloff`, generator.ts:57-63), so the edges become water or beach and the map reads as an island. `carve()` (68-81) digs lakes (`config.lake.count` lakes at 0.22-0.37*size from the centre, 84-108) and one wandering river from edge to edge (111-129), but never inside clearRadius+3 of the centre. Inside clearRadius (6) the elevation is clamped to 0.42-0.62 and moisture to ≤0.5 (140-143), which keeps a meadow around the town hall. Each tile's biome is the FIRST rule in biomes.json whose `when` ranges match (13-23, 150-153). Features (tree/rock/reeds) are rolled once per tile from biome.features chances outside clearRadius (159-168). ensureStartingResources (177-227) then paints a tree patch and a rock patch near the centre if fewer than 10 trees or 4 rocks are within 9 tiles.

Tiles live in WorldMap (world-map.ts:37-58) as flat typed arrays indexed `y*width+x`: biome Uint8 (an index into content.biomes), elevation Uint8 0..255, feature Uint8 (FEATURES = [null,'tree','rock','reeds'], line 11) and occupant Int32 (building uid). A `dirty` Set records the tiles the player changed (only clearing a feature does that, via setFeature 82-87). The server stores every tile. toRows/fromRows (128-168) convert to TileRow [x,y,biomeId,elevation,feature]. toCreatePayload POSTs all W*H rows (serializer.ts:159-171). WorldStore upserts them in chunks of 800 into `epochs_tiles`, one DB row per tile (migration 2026_10_06_100000 lines 52-61; WorldStore.php:136-151), and later syncs send only dirty tiles (serializer.ts:183).

Pathfinding (pathfinding.ts) is a 4-neighbour A* over the grid with a binary MinHeap and Map-based cost/cameFrom. Step costs (86-108): road 0.4; occupied tile = wall unless in `allowed`; unwalkable biome = wall; otherwise 1/biome.speed plus +0.8 for a tree or +2 for a rock. The heuristic is manhattan*0.4. maxNodes defaults to 6000 (300 for strolls, npcs.ts:487-492). When no path is found, the NPC teleports to its target (npcs.ts:465-471).

Terrain (scene/terrain.ts) is ONE height-field mesh with SUB=2 vertices per tile edge, (2W+1)*(2H+1) vertices with alternating diagonals (158-188). tileHeight (38-56) maps biome+elevation to a height: water -0.32 (biome.water) or -0.55 (deep); land max(0,e-0.34)*0.8 plus (e-0.7)*2.2 above 0.7; mountain +0.55+(e-0.84)*9, about 3.2 at most. Corner heights are the average of the up-to-4 adjacent tiles (computeNatural 269-315). heightAt samples them bilinearly and adds valueNoise roughness on mountains and hills (341-348). flatten() (382-431) averages building base heights into the corners under footprints, rewrites every vertex position, recomputes normals, and rebuilds the skirt and the grid. recolor() (468-563) gives each tile a colour from the biome's two colours plus a hash, then darkens water, adds snow to mountain peaks, applies the season tint and snow, and darkens and desaturates land OUTSIDE the territory (×0.6, 511-521). Tile colours are then bilinear-upsampled to the vertices.

The 'floating slab' look comes from five things together. (1) buildSkirt (565-641) builds explicit vertical walls along all 4 map edges, from heightAt down to SKIRT_BOTTOM = -1.4 (line 21), coloured earth #6b5136 / deep #3d2c1c, or #3b7fb0 where water meets the edge. (2) The water is a PlaneGeometry(map.width, map.height) exactly the size of the map (216-223), so the sea stops at the edge. (3) The sky is a screen-space 2x256 gradient CanvasTexture set as scene.background (sky.ts:45-52, 166-193), so nothing lies beyond the slab. (4) Fog only starts at distance*1.6 and ends at distance*5.5+30 (sky.ts:145-154), which at the default distance of 30 is about 195 units, so a 72-tile map's edge is fully visible. (5) The orbit camera target is clamped only to [0,W]x[0,H] (camera.ts:49-59) and can zoom out to 120 (camera.ts:9), so you can always look at the rim.

Water is drawn as a transparent (opacity 0.84, depthWrite false, renderOrder 1) MeshStandardMaterial plane at WATER_Y = -0.1. It has a procedural 128² normal map (waterNormalMap 73-112) scrolled each frame, and its colour comes from the water biome and is lerped to white in snowy seasons (terrain.ts:449-459). Water tiles simply sink below the plane. Roads over open water become bridges (roads.ts:285-303 sets terrain.setDecks; surfaceAt 356-364 raises walkers to DECK_Y).

Features render through scene/features.ts as instanced meshes, one per kind (conifer, leafy trunk, leafy crown, rock dodecahedron, merged reed blades), placed with stable per-tile hashes (157-305). Features.sync compares a full copy of map.feature every 0.5 s (renderer.ts:356-359) and rebuilds when anything changed. Features also drive gameplay. canPlace refuses tiles that hold one (game.ts:836-842). clear() (game.ts:1148-1182) charges world.json economy.clearFeature in gold and pays wood or stone. Production gets `near` bonuses (lumber trees; quarry, mine and coal_mine rocks; game.ts:795-805, 1767-1776, 1905-1909). overlays.ts:536-555 highlights those tiles, and the workshop MapPreview renders generator output (previews.tsx:265-330).

Territory is a SQUARE centred on the map: game.territory() (game.ts:298-314) uses the era's `territory` half-width from epochs.json (8,10,12,14,17,20,23,27,31,35). It is shown three ways: darker ground outside it (recolor), a dashed glowing yellow border with a curtain plus a faint white border for the NEXT era's square (buildBorders 684-726, addBorder 729-790), and a white tile grid over the territory while placing (buildGrid 643-682, toggled in renderer.ts:339-341). inTerritory gates canPlace and clear.

The camera (scene/camera.ts) is a damped orbit camera with yaw/pitch/distance. Pitch is 20-85°, distance 6-120, vfov 40, far plane = distance*6+160 (408-409). It offers zoom-towards-pointer, pan by screen pixels, rotate and tilt. Touch input lives in EpochsGame.tsx:870-1040: one finger pans after a 5 px threshold, two fingers pinch-zoom, rotate and pan at once, right/middle/shift-drag rotates and tilts, and the wheel zooms. There is no tilt gesture or inertia on touch. Picking (renderer.ts groundAt 753-829) ray-marches in 0.1 steps between y = 4 and y = -0.7 and returns null outside [0,W)x[0,H).

## Requirement impact
### R1 [moderate]
Replace generateMap (generator.ts:25-175) with a deterministic buildFixedMap(content). Do not keep random seeds. Read the layout from content, e.g. world.json `map: { width: 100, rows: [{biome:'mountain_foot', count: M}, {biome:'meadow', count: 40}, {biome:'beach', count: 20}, {biome:'sea', count: S}], playable: {x0:0, x1:99, y0:M, y1:M+39}, start: {width: 30} }`. A FIXED-seed low-amplitude noise (or none) can supply gentle relief. Game.create (game.ts:147-152) must stop rolling seeds for the map; state.seed can stay for event RNG (game.ts:132). The town hall must sit at the centre of the PLAYABLE rect, not the whole grid: game.ts:181-187 uses map.height/2, which is wrong once mountain/sand/sea rows are in the grid. Start x = 49, y = M+19 for the 2x2 town_center. Rewrite territory() (game.ts:298-314) from a square era radius to a horizontal strip: {minX: 35-leftCols, maxX: 64+rightCols, minY: M, maxY: M+39}, with new WorldState fields (e.g. land: {left: number, right: number} or purchased plot ids) and an expand action with a cost formula. Drop epochs.json `territory`. buildBorders' 'next era' border (terrain.ts:688-712) becomes 'next purchasable plot' on each side. Persistence gets much simpler: the map is now a pure function of content, so the tiles no longer need saving. Drop TileRow/toRows/fromRows/dirty (world-map.ts:34-35, 128-168; serializer.ts:73-91, 162-189, 212-214, 246) and the epochs_tiles table / upsertTiles / tilesRule in the v3 server copy. world.width/height/seed validation (WorldPayloadRules.php:29-31) becomes a map version string. Recolor's outside-territory darkening (terrain.ts:511-521) and addBorder work on any rect and can be reused unchanged. Also update the registry (registry.ts:258-270) and ContentValidator.php:91-95 (map size checks), and the workshop MapPreview (previews.tsx:265-330).

### R2 [moderate]
(a) Delete buildSkirt and its call sites (terrain.ts:225-232, 428, 562, 565-641; SKIRT_BOTTOM line 21). (b) Make the sea infinite. Replace PlaneGeometry(map.width,map.height) (terrain.ts:216-223) with a very large plane (e.g. 3000x3000, normal-map repeat scaled to match) or a plane that follows the camera target with world-space UVs. (c) Add a new 'Backdrop' module that continues the bands beyond the grid: a low-res ground apron to the left and right of x∈[0,100] (land, sand) and under the sea, coloured like the bands and permanently in the 'locked' tint. Behind the top edge, a mountain ridge mesh: 1D fbm along x, peaks 8-20 units, snow by height, two parallax layers, spanning x≈-600..700. Everything must reach at least fog.far from any allowed camera position: fog far is up to d*5.5+30 = 690 at d=120 (sky.ts:148-153), and camera.far = d*6+160 (camera.ts:409). (d) Because the vertical FOV is 40° and MIN_PITCH is 20° (camera.ts:11), the true horizon is never below the top of the screen. The sky only shows where the ground ENDS, so a ground apron that reaches full fog guarantees no visible edge. scene.background is a screen-space gradient (sky.ts:51) while fog colour = horizon colour (sky.ts:154). Fogged ground must fade into the matching colour, so either keep fog fully saturated before the apron ends or switch to a sky dome / scene.background colour equal to fog.color at low pitch. (e) Clamp the camera to the playable strip plus margins, not [0,W]x[0,H]: setBounds/clampTarget (camera.ts:49-59) should take a rect {minX,maxX,minZ,maxZ}, e.g. x∈[-5,105], z∈[M-2, M+40+20+5]. Optionally lower MAX_DISTANCE (camera.ts:9), or tie fog.far to a constant, to bound the backdrop size. (f) The ground in the grid should stay roughly flat at the outer columns (x=0 and x=99) so the apron meets it seamlessly; heightAt already clamps outside the grid (terrain.ts:317-338), so the apron can sample the edge heights.

### R3 [moderate]
Remove features entirely. Delete scene/features.ts and its wiring in renderer.ts (19, 57, 104, 156, 176, 186, 200, 224, 292, 349, 356-359), the FEATURES/featureCode/feature array/featureAt/setFeature (world-map.ts:11, 21-23, 42, 56, 76-87), Game.clear and the bulldoze clear fallback (game.ts:1148-1182; EpochsGame.tsx ≈791-793), canPlace's feature check (game.ts:836-842), stepCost's feature penalty (pathfinding.ts:105-107), economy.clearFeature (world.json:19-32), biome.features (biomes.json and types.ts:132-136), countFeatureNear and the effects.near feature bonus (game.ts:795-805, 1767-1776, 1905-1909; overlays.ts:536-555; inspector.tsx:79, 376-379), and the 'treeTint' foliage material if no trees remain (renderer.ts:334). Reduce biomes to a few fixed bands: mountain (unbuildable and unwalkable; ideally only the first 1-3 foot rows live in the grid, and the rest is backdrop mesh), meadow/land (buildable), beach/sand (buildable or not, a design choice), sea (water:true, so nearWater for fishery/port works, since canPlace checks biome.water at game.ts:864). Deep water should also get water:true or be merged. Stone mines and quarries need a new placement rule such as placement.nearBiome: {biome:'mountain', radius:1}, generalising the nearWater code (game.ts:851-872), or simply 'must touch row M'. Their production bonus could become 'mountain tiles within radius' instead of rock count. Tall mountain meshes must stay outside picking: groundAt only marches between y=4 and -0.7 (renderer.ts:770-771) and returns null outside the map (818-825). That is fine if the ridge is a separate mesh outside the grid; otherwise raise `top`. The visible band layout (≈20 sand + ≈30 visible water) is mostly backdrop. Only a few sea rows next to the sand need to be real tiles, for ports and piers.

### R4 [moderate]
Terrain side only: multi-lane avenues with sidewalks, rail and an airport strip are much easier on near-flat land. Keep playable heights roughly 0..0.3 and drop the hills/mountain roughness inside the strip (computeNatural rough, terrain.ts:281-287), so road meshes need no slope handling. Wider roads (2-4 tiles) need the ground flattened under them. flatten() takes Footprint[] from buildings.footprints() (renderer.ts:290); check that road tiles are included or pass road segments too. Bridges/decks (roads.ts:285-303, terrain.setDecks) are only needed for piers now that rivers and lakes are gone.

### R5 [moderate]
An airport needs a large flat footprint (e.g. 6x12), which flatten() supports for any rect. A long rail line across the 100-wide strip could run along the land/sand boundary or on a dedicated band row; it is a road-like mask, so the A* walls would need a crossing rule (pathfinding.ts:95-103). A metro is underground and needs a ground-transparency or x-ray view mode. The terrain material is opaque MeshStandardMaterial (terrain.ts:190-198), so add an 'underground' toggle that fades the terrain (opacity or a clipping plane) plus an overlay of tunnels/stations. A harbour or port can use the sea rows (nearWater).

### R6 [trivial]
No terrain impact. Season recolor is keyed by season id and era (terrain.ts:437-446), not by year. If eras no longer jump the calendar, recolor still fires on era change only because the key includes game.state.epoch; that is harmless, and the epoch can be removed from the key if borders no longer depend on the era.

### R7 [trivial]
Terrain-related currency use is limited to world.json economy.clearFeature costs in 'gold' (19-32) and Game.clear, both removed with R3. Land expansion becomes a new money sink priced in the single main currency.

### R8 [moderate]
The map feeds the economy through biome.fertility (farms, game.ts:1702-1712, used in the seasonal output at 1910-1912), the near-feature production bonuses (game.ts:1905-1909) and nearWater placement. In a fixed banded map these become designed constants: land fertility per band, a mountain-adjacency yield for quarries/mines (e.g. yield = base * min(cap, adjacentMountainTiles * k)), sea adjacency for fishery/port, beach for tourism. Land expansion maths for the 7 plots of 5 columns per side (35 per side): e.g. cost(n) = C0 * g^n with n = plots already bought, optionally scaled by population or land value. Any land-value field (distance to centre, sea, mountain, metro) can be computed once from the fixed map.

### R9 [moderate]
Camera and touch for a wide 100x40 strip. The default yaw π/4 (camera.ts:39) should become ~0 (looking north at the mountains, which also hide the far edge), perhaps limited to ±45-60° or 4 snap angles, so the backdrop only needs to cover the front and the sides. The pinch gesture currently mixes zoom, rotate and pan (EpochsGame.tsx:916-925), which causes accidental rotation on phones. Add a rotation dead-zone or a two-finger vertical tilt, pan inertia, and a larger tap threshold than 5 px (≈EpochsGame.tsx:943). Clamp distance and pitch for mobile, e.g. MAX_DISTANCE ~70. groundAt's 0.1-step march from t0 to t1 (renderer.ts:770-789) can take thousands of heightAt calls per pointer move at low pitch and long distance. Intersect the ray with the playable rect / the y∈[0,0.5] slab first: cheap on a near-flat map. A minimap of the strip, showing unlocked and buyable plots, becomes useful and is easy to do because the map is fixed.

## Risks
- Hard-coded geometry assumptions:
  - Territory is a square around (W/2, H/2) (game.ts:298-314).
  - The town hall is placed at the grid centre (game.ts:181-187; EpochsGame.tsx:326-329, 527-530, 1190-1192 also fall back to map.width/2, height/2). If the grid gains mountain, sand or sea rows, every one of these lands in the wrong place.
- Map size range checks: 16..256 in registry.ts:258-270, ContentValidator.php:91-95 and WorldPayloadRules.php:30-31. The factory uses 96x96 (WorldFactory.php:24-25). Tests post 16x16 tile payloads (EpochsWorldTest.php:44-95). All need updating or removing when tiles stop being persisted.
- WorldMap.fromRows leaves missing tiles at biome index 0 = deep_water (Uint8Array zero-init, world-map.ts:153-165). content.biomeIndexOf maps an unknown id to the LAST biome (registry.ts:105-107). Changing the biome order or ids breaks old saves, so v3 must not store tiles at all.
- isWater() (world-map.ts:110-115) returns true for mountains too (any !walkable), and nearWater checks only biome.water (game.ts:864), so deep_water does not count as water. Give the new sea band water:true.
- noise.ts valueNoise reads its grid outside [0,size] and produces NaN; generator.ts:99 samples moistureNoise(x*3, y*3), and lakes can reach negative x. Latent bug, irrelevant if the generator is replaced.
- Floating-slab fix pitfalls:
  - scene.background is screen-space, so at low pitch with mountains a seam can appear between the fog colour and the gradient. Prefer a sky dome or colour-matched background.
  - Fog far grows with distance (sky.ts:149-150), so the backdrop must cover about 700 units at MAX_DISTANCE 120, or fog/zoom must be capped.
  - The water plane is transparent with depthWrite false (terrain.ts:205-214). A huge transparent plane is cheap but sorts against the other transparent overlays (renderOrder 1/2).
  - The shadow camera follows the target (sky.ts:111-127, max box 70), so the far ridge gets no shadows. Bake shading into vertex colours.
- Picking: groundAt marches between y=4 and y=-0.7 (renderer.ts:770-771). Mountains taller than about 3.2 inside the grid would break it. Keep the ridge as a separate non-pickable mesh.
- Performance for ~100 x (M+40+20+S) ≈ 100x80-100 tiles on mobile:
  - The terrain mesh is ≈201x201 = 40k vertices and 80k triangles: fine.
  - flatten() rewrites all positions, recomputes all normals and rebuilds skirt and grid on every structural change (terrain.ts:382-431). Acceptable, but a dirty-rect update would help on phones.
  - recolor() runs over all vertices, but only on season/era change.
  - Roads.sync scans the whole map every 0.25 s with Map lookups (roads.ts:231-250), and the district overlay loops the whole map (overlays.ts:708-719). Restrict both to the playable rect.
  - A* uses Map objects and allocates `{...options, allowed}` for every neighbour (pathfinding.ts:163). Switch to typed arrays plus a generation counter. maxNodes 6000 may fail for cross-town trips on a 100-wide strip; failed paths fall back to teleporting (npcs.ts:465-471).
  - Removing Features saves up to 5 instanced draw calls and the 0.5 s full-array diff.
- Camera pitch: MIN_PITCH 20° equals half the 40° vfov, so at minimum pitch the top screen ray is horizontal and reaches the far plane. That is the worst case for both the backdrop and groundAt cost.
- Expansion left and right only: the darker 'locked' tint must not make the side aprons look like a different world. Use the same locked tint for the apron so expansion visibly 'lights up' columns.
- Mountain-adjacent quarry/mine sites exist only along the top edge (≈100 tiles minus those already used). Expansion gates access to them, an intended design lever, but balance it.

## Reusable
- WorldMap's typed-array layout (biome/elevation/occupant, index(), inBounds(), biomeAt(), isOpenWater()) can stay as-is. Remove the feature array and the TileRow serialisation.
- Terrain height field with corner averaging and footprint flattening (computeNatural, sample, heightAt, surfaceAt, baseOf, flatten): solid and generic for any W×H. It already clamps when sampling outside the grid, which helps when stitching a backdrop apron.
- Per-vertex colouring with season tint, snow and the darker 'outside territory' treatment (recolor): works with any rectangular territory, so it is perfect for the left/right strip expansion.
- Territory border renderer (addBorder, dashed glow + curtain) and the placement grid (buildGrid): rect-based, reusable for 'current land' and 'next plot on each side'.
- Water material and the procedural water normal map with scrolling offset and snow tint (waterNormalMap, update()): reuse on a larger, infinite sea plane.
- Bridge deck mechanism (setDecks/DECK_Y): keep for piers if wanted.
- Sky: day/night, sun/moon, hemisphere light, and fog coloured to the horizon. Keep it; only the background, fog range and backdrop change.
- OrbitCamera: damping, zoom-to-pointer, pan in screen pixels, setYaw. Keep it, with rect bounds and yaw limits added.
- Pointer handling with pinch (EpochsGame.tsx): a decent base for mobile.
- Pathfinding MinHeap/A* structure and stepCost semantics: road cheap, buildings as walls with an `allowed` set for home and work.
- core/random mulberry32/hash2 and colors.hash3 for stable per-tile variation without a seed.
